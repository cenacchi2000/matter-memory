## Beyond a rectangular occluder

The user's subsequent request motivates an additional controlled study of **evidence ownership**. Real video from another actor can be clear and apparently compatible while belonging to the wrong voice. The study includes same-emotion and conflicting-emotion donors, fixed identity references, both checkpoints, and dyad-level intervals. It is explicitly exploratory, uses edited pairs, and does not claim to solve conversation tracking or outperform active-speaker baselines. Its complete protocol and per-query decisions are included.

## What cannot honestly be marked complete

No implementation can guarantee a 10/10 review. Natural multi-person conversational data, strong executed active-speaker comparators, a novel persistent association mechanism, and an independent reserve for that new mechanism are not supplied by these edited-pair experiments. Those are outstanding scientific requirements, not replaced by additional figures or claims.
