Reproduction has three levels

1. Inspect: website, PDFs, and stored outputs.

2. Recompute: run audit58.py on the bundled logits, reliability measurements and fitted models. No raw-video inference occurs at this level.

3. Extract again: requires official raw media, pinned encoder weights, expert checkpoints, and the documented execution environment.
